- **Prerequisites — argued:** the tutorial explicitly asks for knowledge, software, configured environments and access codes before the task; how-to adds known bugs. These slots can prevent discovering missing access or dependencies halfway through ([dev/tutorial/tutorial-template.md:27–45](https://github.com/thegooddocsproject/templates/blob/de840b374c57502bf1e99c3ccfe05a858553f99f/tutorial/tutorial-template.md#L27-L45); [how-to/template-how-to.md:12–19](https://github.com/thegooddocsproject/templates/blob/0029b956025c656165d35189bb0567a6d591b5ca/how-to/template-how-to.md#L12-L19); rationale: [dev/tutorial/tutorial-template-guide.md:73–75](https://github.com/thegooddocsproject/templates/blob/de840b374c57502bf1e99c3ccfe05a858553f99f/tutorial/tutorial-template-guide.md#L73-L75)).
