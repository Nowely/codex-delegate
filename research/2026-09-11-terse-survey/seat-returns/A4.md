## 1. Non-English sources

**N1 — ГОСТ Р 2.105-2019, §§5.2.2–5.2.3.** [Full PDF mirror](https://github.com/Denigmma/agent_doc_system/blob/main/RAG/data/Pdfs%20example%20to%20index%20in%20knowledge%20base/GOST_R_2.105-2019.pdf) opened; ЕСКД requirements for technical text.
**Asserted/normative; no reader measurements.** Distinguishes mandatory from permissive wording and prohibits synonymous technical terms for one concept. Useful Russian equivalents of requirement-strength and terminology checks; current amendments were not verified.

**N2 — Ильяхов’s инфостиль / Главред.** [Official explanation](https://glvrd.ru/about/) and [«Пиши, сокращай» book catalogue](https://book.glvrd.ru/) opened; the book itself was not read.
**Asserted method; heuristic proxy only.** Stop-word proportion drives the score; the publisher explicitly says it is not text quality. Passive-voice/stop-word flags are suggestions; exact scoring weights and comprehension validation were not found.

**N3 — Yandex Cloud’s Russian writing guide.** [Official repository chapter](https://github.com/yandex-cloud/docs/blob/master/guides/how-to-write.md) opened: purpose before procedure, consistent terms, imperatives, one fact per sentence.
**Asserted; no outcome measurements.** Its concrete «осуществлять контроль → контролировать» rule supplies the requested verb-based editing practice; 7–15-word advice is a house rule, not validated comprehension evidence.

**N4 — German rule-based writing in practice.** [DACH handbook rules](https://github.com/rfluethi/learn-wp-dach-team/blob/main/skills/handbuch-autor/references/schreibregeln.md) and its standards comparison opened; adaptation of tekom and IEC guidance, not the original tekom publication.
**Argued; no reader measurements.** Active imperatives, a binding glossary and explicit departures from standards make the provenance and limits of German writing rules reviewable.

**N5 — エンジニアのためのテクニカルライティング.** [Japanese book chapter](https://github.com/mochikoAsTech/technical-writing-book/blob/master/articles/writing.re) opened; practical writing instruction with examples.
**Argued; no outcome measurements.** Explicitly helps irrelevant readers stop reading early and declares audience/goals. Successful documentation can reduce reading, so completion or dwell time alone can mislead.

**N6 — 中文技术文档的写作规范.** [Text rules](https://github.com/ruanyf/document-style-guide/blob/master/docs/text.md) and document-structure chapter opened.
**Asserted; no comprehension measurements.** Chinese examples expose ambiguous pronouns, stacked modifiers and mixed-script conventions that English style checks miss; character-count limits remain prescriptions.

## 2. Safety-critical and regulated writing

**S1 — ASD-STE100, Issue 9 (15 January 2025).** [Official publication access](https://asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html), FAQ and AI white paper opened; the full standard requires a free-copy request and was not obtained.
**Asserted/normative; no measured benefit in the opened material.** The actual standard combines writing rules with approved meanings/parts of speech and domain terms; apparent “simple English” is insufficient evidence of compliance.

**S2 — S1000D Issue 4.2.** [Full specification mirror](https://github.com/Scuba4436/asd-s1000d-42/blob/main/S1000D_Issue_4.2.pdf) and pocket guide opened; inspected applicability, preliminary requirements and business-rule sections.
**Asserted/normative; structural checks, not reader-outcome evidence.** Applicability and BREX business rules make document variants and prerequisites explicit. This is an older issue, not a claim about the latest release.

**S3 — FDA patient instructions and medication labels.** [IFU guidance](https://www.fda.gov/media/128446/download) and [label-design guidance](https://www.fda.gov/media/158522/download) opened in full.
**Argued, evidence-informed guidance; no new intervention measurement.** Action-first instructions and concrete safeguards such as avoiding easily confused dose abbreviations connect editing decisions to foreseeable errors.

**S4 — FDA human-factors validation.** [Full guidance, §§8.1 and appendices](https://www.fda.gov/media/80481/download) opened; procedure/device evaluation protocol.
**Argued protocol, not a reported efficacy trial.** Recommends at least 15 participants per distinct user population, realistic training, observed critical tasks and investigation of errors/near misses. Correct verbal answers alone do not establish safe execution.

**S5 — EU medicinal-leaflet readability guideline, linked by EMA.** [Full Commission guideline](https://health.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2016-11/2009_01_12_readability_guideline_final_en_0.pdf) opened; guidance implementing patient-consultation requirements.
**Asserted test criterion, not an effect size.** Its example targets 90% finding information and 90% of those understanding it, operationalized as 16/20 per question; results cannot be aggregated. Separate findability, understanding and appropriate action.

**S6 — IEC/IEEE 82079-1:2019.** [Official catalogue](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/29075) opened; full paid standard not opened.
**Asserted/normative; catalogue only.** The catalogue explicitly places empirical evaluation and information-production processes in the normative part. This expands review beyond polishing individual sentences.

**S7 — Degani & Wiener, Human Factors of Flight-Deck Checklists: The Normal Checklist.** [Opened excerpts and NASA reference](https://github.com/0xDBFB7/hargrave/blob/9092c8db193d7701369b7f878284fe3d7debac1d/meta_documentation/checklists.md); **excerpt-only**, original report not opened.
**Argued in the inspected excerpts; measurements unverified.** Checklist design includes crew coordination and resistance to becoming a dumping ground for administrative demands—dimensions an isolated reader cannot exercise.

## 3. Instructional design and learning science

**L1 — Kalyuga et al., The Expertise Reversal Effect (2003).** [Opened explanatory chapter](https://github.com/gvwilson/teachtogether.tech/blob/master/en/models.tex) and bibliography identify the Educational Psychologist paper; original paper not opened.
**Argued synthesis of measured human research; effect sizes not audited.** Guidance that helps novices can impede experts. Stratify prior knowledge; a fresh model context does not establish human-like novice status.

**L2 — Worked examples, fading and cognitive load.** [Opened Cognitive Architecture chapter](https://github.com/gvwilson/teachtogether.tech/blob/master/en/architecture.tex), including worked algebra/programming examples and research references.
**Argued research synthesis; no new measurements.** Progress from complete examples through missing steps to independent transfer tasks. Resetting the reader after each question prevents evaluation of this learning trajectory.

## 4. Legal drafting and plain language

**C1 — (Re) Re-visiting false consensus in legal interpretation.** [Study repository](https://github.com/madiganbrodsky/vague_contracts_syntax) opened: README, stimuli, analysis code and anonymized data.
**Measured:** 5,096 raw trial records, 364 raw participant IDs before exclusions; interpretations, confidence and estimated agreement. The syntax pilot varies passive voice/center embedding; I did not re-estimate its effects.
**Largest method change (check c):** use several independent readers per question and retain interpretation distributions alongside the code-derived correctness key. Calibrate model readers against humans: one answer cannot reveal disagreement, and the cited replication’s abstract reports unreliable zero-shot LLM consensus predictions.

**C2 — Accord Project Ergo.** [Compiler](https://github.com/accordproject/ergo) and late-delivery contract template opened; executable contract logic with explicit conditional clauses and penalty caps.
**Argued engineering approach; no reader-comprehension measurements.** Useful for testing obligations, exceptions and boundary cases, while exposing the separate question of whether prose communicates the same semantics as code.

**C3 — The Plain Contract.** [Version 1.0 DOCX](https://github.com/jackmorgan/the-plain-contract/blob/master/The%20Plain%20Contract%201.0.docx) opened; a plain-language freelance agreement with customization examples.
**Asserted; no comprehension measurements.** Concrete payment, ownership and termination clauses provide material for testing whether simpler rewrites preserve obligations, conditions and deadlines.

## 5. Evaluating agents’ ability to follow documents

**A1 — τ³-bench / τ-Knowledge.** [Current repository](https://github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench) and evaluation/retrieval documentation opened; banking documents and domain policies guide conversational actions. Paper unavailable.
**Measured benchmark; not rerun.** `no_knowledge`, `full_kb` and `golden_retrieval` controls distinguish prior knowledge, retrieval and use. Inspect reward components: equivalent final state need not prove every policy was followed; banking grading changed at v1.0.1.

**A2 — Apple ToolSandbox.** [Full README](https://github.com/apple/ToolSandbox) opened; stateful tool-documentation use with intermediate milestone evaluation and tool/argument-name scrambling.
**Measured benchmark; not rerun.** Scrambling forces reliance on docstrings; milestone order tests dependencies. This offers a stronger behavioral probe than asking an agent to paraphrase instructions.
